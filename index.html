01About

I am a PhD student in Industrial Data Science & Engineering at the Department of Industrial Engineering, Pusan National University, working in the Risk Analytics Lab with Prof. Dowon Kim.

My research is on optimization: mathematical models and heuristic algorithms, and more recently reinforcement learning for combinatorial problems. I apply these methods to container terminal operations, vehicle routing, and energy systems.

Before graduate school, I spent two years at the Korea Energy Economics Institute supporting electricity policy research, and I hold a Bachelor of Economics from Soongsil University.

02Publications

International Journals

Preemptive scheduling of a single yard crane for terminal efficiency
Jeongheon Kim*, Wonbeen Park*, Dowon Kim (*co-first authors)
Annals of Operations Research (2026)
DOI: 10.1007/s10479-026-07474-6
Summary
Studies yard crane scheduling in an automated container terminal where vehicle arrivals concentrate at limited input/output points. The crane may interrupt a move, stage the container temporarily, and serve another request first. We formulate a mixed-integer model that trades off makespan against vehicle waiting and storage delays.
Revisiting the total cost of ownership incorporating inconvenience costs
Wonbeen Park, Jongwoo Kim, Taeyoung Jin, Dowon Kim
Research in Transportation Business & Management, 68, 101801 (2026)
DOI: 10.1016/j.rtbm.2026.101801
Summary
Cost comparisons of zero-emission vehicles usually focus on purchase price and running costs. This paper proposes an extended total cost of ownership (TCO) framework that explicitly incorporates the inconvenience costs of charging and refueling, and uses it to evaluate the cost competitiveness of battery electric vehicles (BEVs) and fuel cell electric vehicles (FCEVs) in South Korea.
Evaluating global carbon neutrality commitments: An integrated assessment model approach to the 2 °C target
Dowon Kim, Wonbeen Park, Taeyoung Jin
Environmental Science & Policy, 174, 104280 (2025)
DOI: 10.1016/j.envsci.2025.104280
Summary
Uses the RICE integrated assessment model with the climate pledges of 184 countries to compare business-as-usual, NDC, net-zero, and 1.5 °C scenarios. Current targets leave long-run warming above 2 °C, and the study estimates the additional near-term reductions needed and discusses cooperative governance to limit free-riding.

A study on the estimation of optimal ESS capacity for target curtailment rates in Jeju through an economic dispatch model
Seongjun Lee, Jueun Lee, Wonbeen Park, Seola Yun, Juyeon Kim, Dowon Kim
Journal of Environmental Policy and Administration, 32(4), 167–199 (2024) · in Korean

03Presentations

International Conferences

Deep reinforcement learning with penalties for vehicle routing with backhauls
Wonbeen Park, Dowon Kim
2026 INFORMS Annual Meeting, San Francisco, CA, USA · Nov 2026
Summary
A neural routing policy for the VRP with backhauls that prices precedence-violating arcs by their repair cost through the depot, trained with imitation learning and policy-gradient fine-tuning on instances of up to 500 customers. A single model comes within 5% of one-minute PyVRP runs in under a second per instance and scales to instances an order of magnitude larger than those seen in training.
